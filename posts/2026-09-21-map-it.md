# Map it

### Published: 21 September 2026

## Spatial data visualisation
![Spatial](/images/chart2.png)

I chose to do a dot density map showing the road crashes in Victoria from 2012 to 2016. <br><br>

I set the background colour to be grey as I was going for a dark themed visualisation. Then I loaded the victorian road crash data and dropped the rows with no coordinates. Then I filtered the crashes down to only fatal and serious injury crashes. <br>
Then I plotted the points such that serious injury crashes were gold and slightly smaller and the fatal injury crashes were red and larger. <br><br>

I then added the title and a small note saying each dot represents a single crash. <br><br>

After plotting this I realised the border was only an outline of Victoria created by the crashes. Since I needed a proper state boundary for my spatial graph, I used geopandas and loaded in the Victoria boundary. (Ashtari, 2021). <br><br>

I accessed Natural Earth 10m Admin-1 States and Provinces dataset retrieved directly visa geopandas.read_file() from natural Earth. I loaded thus as a zipped file using zip+, and then filtered to admin=="Australia" and name=="Victoria" to extract the state boundary. I added this boundary in dark grey color so it would contrast against the red and gold colours of the crashes. <br>
Then I plotted the spatial graph again. Now the crashes appeared inside a clear border of the Victoria state.
