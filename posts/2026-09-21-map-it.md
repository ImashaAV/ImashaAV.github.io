# Map it

### Published: 21 September 2026

## Spatial data visualisation
![Spatial](/images/map.jpeg)

I first loaded the victorian road crash data and dropped the rows with no coordinates. Then I filtered the crashes down to only fatal and serious injury crashes. <br>
Then using geopandas, I loaded in the Victoria boundary (Towards Data Science, n.d.).<br><br>

I set the background colour to be grey as I was going for a dark themed visualisation. I then added the Victoria boundary and plotted the points. The serious injury crashes were gold and slightly smaller and the fatal i jury crashes were red and larger.<br>

I then added the title.
