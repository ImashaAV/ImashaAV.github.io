# Checked and accessible

### Published: 31 August 2026

## Visualisation before improvements
![Recolouring](/images/Recoloured_new.png)

## Visualisation after improvements
![Accessible](/images/Accessible.png)

In this exercise, I examined my reproduced and recoloured visualisation for accessibility issues.  
By examining it myself and by running the through the Data Visualisation Checklist, I identified the below accessibility issues.  
- The yellow colour of the negative temperature range is quite pale.
- The title could be more descriptive
- The axis text sizes are quite small
- Graph doesn't annotate or highlight important findings

The improvements I made:
- I changed the colour scheme into a diverging three colour hue. The colours go from blue to yellow to red. Now, the pale colours don't seem to blend into one another like it did before.
- I annotated the graph with two important findings: Greatest negative temperature change and greatest positive temperature change.
- Since the text size of the axis was quite small, I made them larger.
- The title was made more descriptive and larger in text size
